# Say Halo 0.8.12

Recording feedback now lives in the macOS menu bar, freeing up your browser tabs and address bar.

- See a white-and-blue live waveform and elapsed recording time in the menu bar.
- Stop or cancel a recording directly from the Say Halo menu.
- See quiet speech more clearly, with a steady capsule width and gentle transitions while processing.

Your shortcuts, microphone selection, models, and personal settings stay the same.
