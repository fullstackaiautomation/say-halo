# Say Halo 0.8.9

- Converts spoken numbers and percentages into compact text, including “two hundred and
  twenty-three” → `223` and “ten percent” → `10%`.
- Stops ordinary text edits from being silently learned as global replacements.
- Keeps correction learning available through History's explicit Correct & Learn action, where the
  proposed rules are shown before saving.
- Preserves every user's private Replace words mappings during updates. Custom mappings remain on
  that user's Mac and are never distributed with Say Halo.
